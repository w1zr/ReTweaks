## RYTWEAKS v1.4

### New look
- **New chip logo and startup animation.** The chip draws itself, the pins light up, a scan line sweeps, and the R traces in. If RYTWEAKS loads fast you see a quick version of about 1 second. If it takes longer, the scan keeps going until it's really ready. There's no added waiting.
- **New app icon.** The chip is used for the window, the taskbar and the .exe, and each size is drawn separately so even the small ones stay sharp.
- **Line icons in the sidebar**, a **sliding accent bar** that follows the page you pick, and a short **fade** between pages.
- **About page** with what's new in this version.

### Health Check
- One scan covers:
  - startup apps
  - temp files
  - power plan
  - disk health
  - free space
  - pending restarts
  - Windows Update
  - Game Mode
  - monitor refresh rate
  - RAM
- Each result lights a pin on the chip (green good, amber or red worth a look, grey unavailable).
- **Fix** buttons use RYTWEAKS' existing tools. They always ask first, and cleanup always shows its preview.

### FPS test
- Measures **real average FPS, 1% lows and 0.1% lows** with Intel PresentMon. You download PresentMon yourself from Intel's GitHub, and RYTWEAKS shows you where.
- Runs are saved, so you can compare two of them side by side. Results only compare fairly with the same scene, settings and run length, and RYTWEAKS never credits a tweak automatically.

### Display & drivers
- Shows whether each monitor runs at its **highest refresh rate**. For example, a 165 Hz monitor stuck at 60 Hz is a common cause of choppy games.
- Fix it in one click. Windows-style "Keep these settings?" protection switches back after 15 seconds if you don't confirm.
- Shows your GPU driver version and how old it is.

### AI Assistant (Gemini)
- Ask questions about your PC. It knows your specs if you allow it, and it can point you to the right RYTWEAKS page.
- It uses **your own free Gemini API key**, which is stored in Windows Credential Manager and never in a settings file.
- Google's terms apply: you must be **18 or older**, and free-tier chats may be reviewed by Google. "See what's shared" shows exactly what is sent.
- It gives advice only and never changes anything on its own.

### Audio mixer
- Master volume and mute, output devices, and **per-app volume and mute**. It stays in sync with changes you make in Windows.

### Auto clicker
- Left, right or middle button; single or double clicks; speed in CPS or milliseconds; an optional click limit, time limit, random interval and start delay.
- Start and stop with a hotkey (F6), plus an **emergency stop (F8)** that always works.
- You're responsible for following each game's rules, and many online games ban auto clickers.

### Fun tools
- **Mouse polling rate test**, **clicks-per-second test** and **reaction time test**, all with real measurements. Best scores are saved.

### More
- **Background calming:** while a game runs, RYTWEAKS lowers the CPU priority of only the apps you pick, then puts them back.
- **Sensor logging to CSV** (temperatures, loads and clocks) from the Snapshots page.
- **Disk space analyzer:** see what's using your space. It never deletes anything.
- **Storage Explorer:** breadcrumbs, search, sorting, a hidden-items switch, copy path, open and show in Explorer.
- **Startup apps** now include **scheduled tasks** that run at sign-in or boot. You can disable and re-enable them, and they're never deleted.
- **Game presets:** rename, duplicate, edit, export and import your own presets.

### Good to know
- RYTWEAKS isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen or antivirus may warn you when you download it.
- Your settings, presets and rules from v1.3 are kept.
