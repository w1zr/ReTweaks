# RETWEAKS v2.0.1

A fix for the **AI assistant**. Everything else is the same as [v2.0](https://github.com/w1zr/ReTweaks/releases/tag/v2.0); see its notes for the full list of features.

Website: https://w1zr.github.io/ReTweaks/ · Discord: https://discord.gg/fS5NwW9TPF

## Fixed
- **AI assistant showed "Google HTTP 401: API key not valid".** The shared Gemini key in v2.0 was disabled by Google shortly after release. v2.0.1 fixes it in two ways:
  - **Use your own key:** the AI assistant page has a new **Your Gemini key** box.
    1. Click **Get a free key**; it opens Google AI Studio.
    2. Copy the key and paste it into the box.
    3. Click **Save key**, and the AI reloads with it straight away.
  - **Remove my key** goes back to the built-in key.
  - Your key is stored only on this PC, encrypted for your Windows account, and is sent only to Google. Google's terms require you to be 18 or older.
  - Price checks, driver help and the AI service check use the same key.
  - **Built-in keys:** shared keys are now added when the release is built and are no longer stored in the public source code.
  - If Google says a key no longer works (invalid, disabled or expired), the AI switches to the next key automatically and keeps using the one that works. A key that only reached its usage limit isn't switched; the AI says "limit reached, try again later".
- If Google rejects a key, the AI page now says so and points you to the key box.

## New
- **Choose your Gemini model** on the AI assistant page, like earlier RETWEAKS versions did.
  - The list comes from Google and shows every chat model your key can use, for example Gemini 3.5 Flash or Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite.
  - **Automatic** picks the newest non-preview Flash-Lite model.
  - Your choice is saved, and a model you picked in v1.6 is kept if your key still offers it.
- **See my limits** opens your Google AI Studio rate-limit page. Free-tier limits differ per model and per Google account, so check yours there before choosing.

## Removed
- The **Notifications** page (it only listed recent tweak requests). That history is still saved and still used by the AI assistant.

## Updating
Use **Update now** in the app, or download `RETWEAKS.exe` below. Your settings are kept.

The .exe isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen or antivirus may warn. License: GPL-3.0-only.
