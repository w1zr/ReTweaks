# ReTweaks 2.0.9

**Download:** `RETWEAKS.exe` (or `RETWEAKS.zip`) below. Already have ReTweaks? Click **Update now** in the app. Your settings are kept.

## What's new

- **Changing the language works right away again.** In 2.0.8, choosing a language in Settings could freeze the window for a long time on Windows: the sidebar icons disappeared and the text stayed in the old language.
  - What's on screen (the sidebar, the top bar and the open page) now switches immediately.
  - The other pages switch in the background in small steps, so ReTweaks stays usable.
  - The sidebar icons stay where they are.
  - Text widths are measured once and remembered, which makes switching faster.
- The Dashboard banner menu in Settings is wider, so longer translated names fit.

Full feature list: see the [2.0.5 release](https://github.com/w1zr/ReTweaks/releases/tag/v2.0.5).
