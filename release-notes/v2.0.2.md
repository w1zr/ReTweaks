# RETWEAKS v2.0.2

A smarter, more reliable **AI assistant** and a cleaner app. Everything from [v2.0](https://github.com/w1zr/ReTweaks/releases/tag/v2.0) is still here, and your settings carry over.

Website: https://w1zr.github.io/ReTweaks/ · Discord: https://discord.gg/fS5NwW9TPF

---

## AI assistant

- **Works out of the box.** RETWEAKS has its own built-in Gemini connection, so you don't need an account or key.
- **Stays connected.** RETWEAKS carries more than one AI key. If Google stops accepting one, the assistant switches to the next one by itself and remembers which works, so you can keep chatting.
- **Clear when you've hit a limit.** If you reach Google's usage limit, the assistant tells you to try again later.
- **Choose your model.** A new **Model** dropdown lists every Gemini model available, for example Gemini 3.5 Flash or Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite.
  - **Automatic** picks the newest Flash-Lite model.
  - Your choice is saved, and a model you picked in an earlier version is kept when it's still available.
- **See my limits** opens Google AI Studio's rate-limit page. Limits differ per model, so you can compare before choosing.
- **Nicely formatted replies.** Bold text, italics, headings, bullet points and code now show as real formatting.
- Price checks on the PC Value Checker, driver help and the AI service check use the same connection.

## App

- **Cleaner sidebar:** the Notifications page is gone. Your recent tweak history is still saved, and the AI assistant still uses it for context.
- **New website:** https://w1zr.github.io/ReTweaks/, linked from About, Settings and the Discord status button.
- **Translations** updated for every new text in English, Español, Français, Deutsch and Português (Brasil).

## Updating

Click **Update now** in RETWEAKS, or download `RETWEAKS.exe` (or `RETWEAKS.zip`) below. Your settings, themes, tweak history and backups are kept.

The .exe isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen or antivirus may show a warning. License: GPL-3.0-only.
