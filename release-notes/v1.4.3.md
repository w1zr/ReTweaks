## RYTWEAKS v1.4.3

### Theme maker
- Make your own theme in **Settings → Theme maker**. You pick every color (background, sidebar, cards, borders, text, accent and status colors), see it on the whole app with **Preview**, then **Save** it.
- A readability check warns you if text would be hard to read on your colors.
- **Share your theme:** **Copy share code** gives you a code starting with `RYT1-`. Anyone can paste it into **Import code…**.

### Discord status
- Show **"Optimizing with RYTWEAKS"** on your Discord profile, or **"Gaming: Fortnite"** while a game from Program rules is running, with a **Get RYTWEAKS** button.
- **Off by default.** Turn it on in **Settings → Discord status**. You can hide which game you're playing.
- RYTWEAKS only talks to the Discord app on your PC. The Discord desktop app needs to be running.

### 44 new tweaks (53 in total), with search
The **Tweaks** page has a **search box** (try “sticky keys”, “mouse” or “notifications”) and is now grouped into **Gaming, Privacy, Interface, Services and Maintenance**. Every tweak says what it does, its trade-off, and whether it needs admin rights or a restart, shows its real current state, and has its own **Undo**. Safe tweaks come first in each group; disruptive ones are marked **Advanced**.
- **Gaming and performance:** Store apps in the background, faster menus, window animations, power throttling, CPU reserved for background tasks (MMCSS), notification banners, the Narrator shortcut, Microsoft Edge running in the background, shortest key-repeat delay, opening Game Bar with the controller's Xbox button.
- **Privacy:** advertising ID, tailored experiences, optional diagnostic data, activity history, web results in Start search, online speech recognition, inking & typing personalization, suggested content in Settings, app suggestions in Start, tips notifications, lock screen tips, automatic installs of suggested apps, Recall snapshots (Copilot+ PCs).
- **Interface:** file extensions, hidden files, full right-click menu (Windows 11), taskbar on the left, Task View button, Widgets, dark mode, "End task" in the taskbar, open File Explorer to This PC, seconds in the taskbar clock.
- **Services (Advanced):** Telemetry (Connected User Experiences), SysMain, Windows Search indexing, Print Spooler, Fax, Downloaded Maps Manager, Windows Error Reporting. Turning one off sets it to Disabled after a restart; Undo restores its exact original start type.
- **Maintenance:** Storage Sense, no automatic restart while signed in, drivers from Windows Update, sharing updates with PCs on your network.

### Presets
Ten presets: **Esports, Max FPS, Balanced, Low-end PC, High quality, Ultra quality, Streamer, Privacy**, plus Max Performance and Windows Default. Each preset is a list of individual tweaks it turns on or off, shown on its card. On the **Tweaks** page, pick a preset and press **Select its tweaks**: it ticks every tweak in that preset and sets each to On or Off. Untick any you don't want, tick more (or **Select all shown** after a search), use **Clear selection** to start over, then press **Apply selected**. On the **Profiles** page you can apply a whole preset in one click. Either way RYTWEAKS first reads your current settings and shows only what will actually change, then applies after you confirm. Every change can be undone one by one on the Tweaks page, or all at once with **Undo everything**. No preset promises an FPS gain; check with the FPS test.

### Essentials
On the Tweaks page: **Clear DNS cache**, **Clear Windows Update download cache** (shows the size and asks first) and **Check and repair system files** (Windows' SFC scan, 5–20 minutes).

Everything from v1.4.2 is unchanged. Use the **Update now** bar at the top of RYTWEAKS to update. Your settings are kept.
