## RETWEAKS v1.4.1

### New themes
- **Seven full-UI themes** that recolor the background, sidebar, cards, borders and text:
  - Midnight (default)
  - **Super Black**, pure black for OLED screens
  - **Galaxy**, deep space colors with a starfield behind the pages
  - Ocean
  - Ember
  - Forest
  - **Light**, a real light mode
- The accent themes (Pink, Blue, Purple, Red, Green, Cyan) and the custom HEX accent still work. Pick one in **Settings → Appearance**.

### Animated Dashboard banner
- A banner across the top of the Dashboard, with four styles:
  - **CPU cores:** one tile per CPU thread, filled with its real live load.
  - **Load wave:** your real CPU load over the last minute.
  - **Circuit traces**
  - **Galaxy**
- CPU cores is the default. It only animates while the Dashboard is open, and you can turn it off in Settings.

### Sidebar header
- The small chip logo now sits next to the RETWEAKS wordmark. Prefer the wordmark alone? Choose **Wordmark only** in Settings.


### Sharper app icon
- The `.exe` file icon in File Explorer and on shortcuts is redrawn for small sizes (16–48 px), so the chip outline no longer gets cut off and the R stays crisp.
- The icon on the taskbar and the window while RETWEAKS is running stays exactly the same as in v1.4.
- The dark rounded square stays, so the icon is visible on both light and dark backgrounds.

Everything from v1.4 is unchanged. If you're on v1.4, use the **Update now** bar at the top of RETWEAKS. Your settings are kept.
