## RYTWEAKS v1.4.1

### Sharper app icon
- The chip icon is redrawn for small sizes (16–48 px), so it's crisp in the taskbar, Explorer and shortcuts. Before, the chip outline got cut off and the R looked smeared at small sizes.
- The dark rounded square stays, so the icon is visible on both light and dark backgrounds.

Everything from v1.4 is unchanged. If you're on v1.4, use the **Update now** bar at the top of RYTWEAKS. Your settings are kept.
