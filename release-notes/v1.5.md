## RYTWEAKS v1.5

### Browser
A new **Browser** page (Tools section of the sidebar): the RYTWEAKS browser, with the chip logo and RYTWEAKS name in its bar, built on Microsoft's Edge engine, which is part of Windows 11 (on Windows 10 it comes with Microsoft Edge). It adds only a few MB to RYTWEAKS.
- **RYTWEAKS start page** in every new tab: the logo, a search box, and your bookmarks as tiles.
- **Follows your theme:** the browser bar and start page use your RYTWEAKS theme (Galaxy even gets its starfield), and websites with a dark mode use it on dark themes.
- **Tabs:** open, close, drag to reorder. Links that open a new window open in a new tab.
- **Address bar that also searches.** Pick your search engine in the **•••** menu: Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Bing, Startpage, or your own search address.
- **Private tabs:** no history, cookies or cache are kept after the tab closes.
- **Bookmarks** (☆ or Ctrl+D) and **History** (Ctrl+H), both searchable. **Clear history** and **Clear cookies and site data** are in the ••• menu.
- **Downloads** (Ctrl+J): choose the folder, or have it ask every time; open the file or show it in its folder.
- Shortcuts: Ctrl+T new tab, Ctrl+W close tab, Ctrl+L address bar, Ctrl+Shift+N private tab, F5 reload.
- Nothing starts until you open the Browser page. Bookmarks and history are saved in `%APPDATA%\RYTWEAKS\browser.json`, separate from your RYTWEAKS settings.

### Microphone and volume lock in the Audio mixer
- The Audio mixer now has a **Microphone** section next to the speakers: microphone volume and mute (the same switches as Windows Sound settings → Input), and your input devices with **Set as default**.
- **Lock** on the speaker and microphone sliders: keeps the volume where you set it. If an app or Windows changes it (for example Discord's automatic mic adjustment), RYTWEAKS puts it back within half a second. The microphone lock follows whichever mic is your default. Mute is never touched.

### Fortnite stretched resolution
**Game presets → Fortnite → Stretched resolution:** pick 1720×1080, 1680×1080, 1650×1080, 1600×1080 or 1440×1080 and press **Apply stretched**. RYTWEAKS backs up Fortnite's settings file, sets the resolution and Fullscreen mode (only settings already in the file), and can lock the file so Fortnite doesn't reset it. **Back to native** puts your screen's own resolution back. Your graphics card must be set to stretch (Full-screen / Full panel scaling) or Fortnite shows black bars; the steps for NVIDIA, AMD and Intel are on the page. Epic locks competitive modes (Arena and tournaments) to 16:9, so stretched only applies in regular matches.

### Keep running in the tray
**Settings → Behavior → Keep running in the system tray when I close the window** (off by default). Closing the window then hides RYTWEAKS next to the clock, so the volume lock and auto game mode keep working. Right-click the tray icon → **Quit RYTWEAKS** to close it fully. With "Start with Windows" and "Start minimized" on, RYTWEAKS starts straight in the tray.

RYTWEAKS stays free and open source. Everything from v1.4.4 is unchanged. Use the **Update now** bar at the top of RYTWEAKS to update. Your settings are kept.
