# ReTweaks 2.0.5

Two fixes on top of [2.0.4](https://github.com/w1zr/ReTweaks/releases/tag/v2.0.4).

Website: https://w1zr.github.io/ReTweaks/ · Discord: https://discord.gg/fS5NwW9TPF

## Fixed
- **Dashboard banner animates from the first launch.** The animated banner now starts as soon as the ReTweaks window appears, without switching tabs. It also resumes by itself after the window is restored from minimized or the tray.
- **Browser search engine list stays inside ReTweaks.** The Search engine list in the browser menu now opens to the left when there isn't room on the right, so it no longer appears on another monitor.

## Updating
Click **Update now** in ReTweaks, or download `RETWEAKS.exe` (or `RETWEAKS.zip`) below. Your settings are kept.

The .exe isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen or antivirus may show a warning. License: GPL-3.0-only.
