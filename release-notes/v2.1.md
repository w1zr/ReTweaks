# ReTweaks 2.1

**Download:** `RETWEAKS.exe` (or `RETWEAKS.zip`) below. Already have ReTweaks? Click **Update now** in the app. Your settings are kept.

## What's new

- **Smoother everywhere.**
  - With the volume lock on, the window no longer stutters twice a second. The lock now works in the background.
  - Switching pages is about twice as fast, and pages appear in one go instead of showing black boxes while they draw.
- **Fixed a crash in Fun tools.** Opening Fun tools a second time could close ReTweaks.
- **Welcome tour:** "Step 5 of 8" is now translated in every language.

Full feature list: see the [2.0.5 release](https://github.com/w1zr/ReTweaks/releases/tag/v2.0.5).
