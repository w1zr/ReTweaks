# RETWEAKS v2.0.3

A small polish update on top of [v2.0.2](https://github.com/w1zr/ReTweaks/releases/tag/v2.0.2).

Website: https://w1zr.github.io/ReTweaks/ · Discord: https://discord.gg/fS5NwW9TPF

## Fixed
- The startup screen's loading messages ("Loading Settings…" and others) now show the "…" correctly instead of odd characters.
- The AI assistant's status messages ("Connecting to Gemini…", "Sending…", "Waiting for Gemini…") show correctly too.

## Updating
Click **Update now** in RETWEAKS, or download `RETWEAKS.exe` (or `RETWEAKS.zip`) below. Your settings are kept.

The .exe isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen or antivirus may show a warning. License: GPL-3.0-only.
